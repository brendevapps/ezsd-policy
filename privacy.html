This Privacy Policy applies to the EzSD mobile application ("App"), developed and published by BrenDevApps ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our").

1. Information We Collect and Process

Local File & Storage Access: EzSD is an on-device file and external storage (OTG/SD card) utility. When you grant storage or USB/OTG access permissions, the App reads, copies, moves, or manages files strictly locally on your device and connected external drives. We do not upload, transmit, or store your personal files, photos, videos, or documents on external servers.

Personal Information: EzSD does not require account registration and does not directly collect your name, physical address, or phone number.

We do not sell your personal data. Depending on your use of the App, the following Google Play platform services may process limited technical or diagnostic data:

3. Data Security

All file operations performed by EzSD occur locally on your Android device. Any diagnostic or purchase verification communication handled by Google Play platform services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because EzSD processes your files locally and does not maintain user accounts or remote databases, we do not retain copies of your files. Uninstalling the App removes all local app data from your device. For any privacy-related requests or data deletion inquiries regarding diagnostic logs or support communications, contact us using the email below.

EzSD is a general-audience utility application and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
